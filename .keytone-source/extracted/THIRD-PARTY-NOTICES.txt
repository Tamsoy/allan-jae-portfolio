KeyTone modifications: physical scan-code mapping, extraction of separate
press/release events using the upstream timing definitions, silence trimming,
DC removal, short edge fades, mono conversion, 48 kHz resampling and pack-wide
level adjustment. Unrecorded modifier keys use corresponding recorded keys;
other unsupported physical keys fall back to the recorded A key.
Variation makes small pitch/volume changes to a key's recording.
No affiliation with or endorsement by CHERRY or Mechvibes is implied.
Actual sound varies with keycaps, keyboard case, microphone and speakers.
